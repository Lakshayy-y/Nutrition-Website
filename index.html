01 • ABOUT

Understanding a Healthy Diet

Healthy eating is about choosing a variety of foods that provide the nutrients our bodies need.

01

What is a Healthy Diet?

A healthy diet includes a variety of nutritious foods in suitable amounts. It can include fruits, vegetables, whole grains, protein-rich foods, healthy fats and enough water.

02

What is a Balanced Diet?

A balanced diet means including different food groups so that meals provide a broad range of essential nutrients. Balance and variety are important parts of healthy eating.

03

Why is Balance Important?

Different nutrients perform different roles in the body. Eating a varied diet helps provide the nutrients needed for energy, growth and normal body functions.

02 • ESSENTIAL NUTRIENTS

Building Blocks of Good Nutrition

A balanced diet contains different nutrients and components that support normal body functions.

🌾

Carbohydrates

Provide energy and are found in foods such as grains, fruits, vegetables and legumes.

🥚

Proteins

Help support body tissues and are found in foods such as pulses, eggs, dairy products, nuts and other protein-rich foods.

🥑

Healthy Fats

Fats are an important part of the diet. Sources include nuts, seeds, avocados and certain plant oils.

🍊

Vitamins

Vitamins are micronutrients needed in small amounts for various normal body functions.

🥬

Minerals

Minerals such as calcium, iron and potassium contribute to different normal body processes.

🌱

Fibre

Fibre is found mainly in plant foods such as whole grains, fruits, vegetables, beans and lentils.

💧

Water

Water is essential for normal body functions. Regular hydration is an important part of a healthy lifestyle.

04 • HEALTHY HABITS

Simple Habits for Better Eating

Small, consistent habits can make healthy eating easier to practice every day.

01 🍽️

Eat a Variety of Foods

Choose foods from different groups to add variety to your meals.

02 🥦

Include Fruits & Vegetables

Add different fruits and vegetables throughout the day.

03 🌾

Choose Whole Grains

Include whole-grain foods as part of a varied eating pattern.

04 🥜

Include Protein Foods

Add suitable protein-rich foods such as pulses, eggs, dairy, nuts or seeds.

05 💧

Drink Enough Water

Make water a regular part of your daily routine and stay hydrated.

08 ⚖️

05 • BENEFITS

Why Choose a Balanced Diet?

A balanced eating pattern provides a broad range of nutrients that the body needs for normal functions and everyday activities.

✓

Supports Normal Body Functions

Nutrients contribute to many normal processes in the body.

✓

Provides Essential Nutrients

A varied diet can provide a wide range of vitamins, minerals and other nutrients.

✓

Supports Energy Needs

Carbohydrates, fats and proteins contribute to the body's energy supply and other functions.

✓

Supports Healthy Growth & Development

Adequate nutrition is important during periods of growth and development.

✓

Balanced food choices can help develop practical and sustainable eating habits.
